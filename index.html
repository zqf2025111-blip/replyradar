Frequently asked questions

Why do I need to bring my own OpenAI key?

BYOK means you pay only for the AI you actually use, and we keep our subscription prices low. Your API key is encrypted with AES-256 and never exposed to us or anyone else.

Will I get banned for using this?

No. ReplyRadar uses a "human-in-the-loop" approach — we generate reply suggestions, but you manually post them. This keeps your accounts safe and your replies authentic.

Which platforms do you support?

Reddit and Hacker News on all plans. Twitter/X is available on Pro and above. More platforms (LinkedIn, IndieHackers) are on our roadmap.

How is this different from F5Bot?

F5Bot is free and does basic keyword matching — you still get flooded with noise. ReplyRadar uses AI to understand context and intent, so you only see posts where someone is genuinely looking for a solution like yours.
